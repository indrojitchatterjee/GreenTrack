🌿 GreenTrack – Personal Expense Tracker
**Track your money. Understand your spending. Manage your budget.

GreenTrack is a full-stack personal expense tracker designed to help users manage their **income, salary, daily expenses, and monthly budgets** in one place. It allows users to record transactions, categorize expenses, monitor their balance, and understand where their money is being spent.

## ✨ Key Features

*  User Authentication** – Secure registration and login
*  Income Management** – Add salary and other sources of income
* Expense Tracking** – Record daily expenses
* Expense Categories** – Organize spending by category
* Dashboard** – Get a quick overview of your financial activity
* Balance Tracking** – Automatically calculate income, expenses, and remaining balance
* Monthly Tracking** – Monitor monthly income and spending
* Budget Management** – Set and monitor monthly budgets
* Budget Alerts** – Get notified when spending approaches the budget limit
* Spending Insights** – Understand your spending patterns
